## The CSV section's search fault, on a plain straight

`work/uneven-straight.csv` (made by this script): a level straight of 2,001 points from x = 0 to 200.02 m, spacing shrinking from 0.12 to 0.08 m. `fvdcore import csv work/uneven-straight.csv work/uneven-straight.fvd`, then `fvdcore nodes work/uneven-straight.fvd --every-m 20 --frame anchor`: the last node has dist_m 226.54 m at heart_x 200.02 m. The centre-line length of a straight is overstated by 13.3 %.
