## Experiment A: one camelback, two unknowns, two goals

Element: valley-to-valley camelback, Gp = 3.5 g valley force, t1 = 1.0 s ramps,
0.4 s holds at Gp, cubic transitions; the drop mirrors the climb (t4 = t2).
Goals: exit level (unwrapped pitch 0) and crest (heartline) at a stated height.
Unknowns: t2 (the Gp -> airtime transition) and t_air (airtime hold).
Solver: damped Newton, forward-difference Jacobian (step 0.01 s), backtracking
by halving; tolerance 0.05 deg and 0.05 m; shape guard: |pitch| < 80 deg, no stall.
An 'evaluation' is one integration of the whole element.

## Experiment B: the exit contract in a two-element layout

Layout: camelback E1 (airtime 0.0 g, crest at y = 38 m), camelback E2 (airtime
-0.6 g, crest at y = 28 m), then 2 s of level 1 g run-out. Entry at y = 0.
Each element's contract: exit level at y = 0 m with its crest at the stated
height; unknowns t2, t_air, t4 (three goals, three unknowns).
Baseline entry 30 m/s. Changes: entry speed +3 % and -3 % (the energy changes, as
with a taller lift), and 'same energy, entry 1 m lower' (as with a deeper drop
before the element). Cases: nothing re-solves (the solved numbers are kept);
E1 re-solves its contract; E1 and E2 both re-solve.

Baseline solved in 42 evaluations. E1: t2 0.438, t_air 4.081, t4 0.398 s. E2: t2 0.293, t_air 2.662, t4 0.263 s.

## Experiment C: transition lengths from an onset-rate limit

Every transition is as short as the limit J (g/s) allows: a cubic of change dG
lasts 1.5 |dG| / J seconds (its peak slope is 1.5 dG per unit time). So t1 = t5
= 1.5 (Gp - 1) / J and t2 = t4 = 1.5 (Gp - Ga) / J, holds at Gp 0.4 s. With the
transition lengths no longer free, the unknowns become Gp (valley force) and
t_air; goals: exit level, crest 25 m above the entry. Airtime 0.0 g and -0.6 g.
Starting point: Gp scanned from 1.5 to 5.0 g in 0.25 g steps with t_air solved
for a level exit at each; the valid one with the crest nearest 25 m is the start
(those evaluations included). Then damped Newton.
