# Glossary

The terms as the code uses them. File references point at where the term is defined or computed. The map is `docs/codebase-map.md`.

- **FVD, force vector design.** Designing track by prescribing the forces the rider feels over time and integrating the geometry that produces them, instead of drawing the shape and reading the forces off it. In the code this is a *forced section* (`secforced`): the designer gives the normal and lateral G as functions of time or distance, and `secforced::updateSection()` (`core/secforced.cpp`) rotates the train's direction each millisecond so that those forces plus gravity are exactly what the frame feels. newton2 by Keith "Entropy" Cohn is the tool FVD++ reimplements.
- **Section (segment).** One piece of the chain that makes up a track: straight, curved, forced, geometric, Bézier or NoLimits CSV (`enum secType`, `core/section.h`). The UI calls them segments in places (`appendSegment`, `changeSegmentLength`). Each section starts at the previous section's last node.
- **Node.** One sample of the train state, 1/1000 s apart in train time (`F_HZ` in `core/mnode.h`): position, frame vectors, roll, speed, energy and the felt forces (`mnode`). A 10-second section holds about 10,000 nodes.
- **Anchor.** The track's starting node: position, yaw, pitch, roll, speed and the initial normal and lateral force (`track::anchorNode`, edited in the anchor frame of `ui/trackwidget.cpp`). Since v0.14 it is the anchor of the track, not of the heartline.
- **Spine and heartline.** The **heartline** is the path of the rider's heart, and it is what the program computes: `mnode::vPos` is a point on the heartline. The track centre line (the spine, the rails) lies `fHeart` metres from it along the node's normal, at `mnode::vPosHeart(fHeart)`; the function's name is misleading, it returns the track point, not the heart (`track::fHeart`; checked against the code on 2026-11-10, see `docs/research/r1-heartlines-and-trains.md`, section 1). A positive `fHeart` puts the rails below the rider, a negative one above (a train that hangs). Forces are computed on the heartline; the straight section keeps the heartline length, not the spine length. "Heartlining" an export means writing the spine rather than the heartline (the "Export without heartlining" box in `ui/exportui.ui`).
- **Frame: direction, lateral, normal.** `vDir` points along the track, `vLat` to the rider's right, `vNorm` from the rider down toward the rails (`vNorm = vDir × vLat`, `mnode::updateNorm()`; at the anchor `vDir = (0,0,-1)`, `vLat = (1,0,0)`, `vNorm = (0,-1,0)`). World up is +Y; the track runs toward −Z at zero yaw.
- **Roll (banking).** Rotation of the frame about `vDir`, in degrees (`mnode::fRoll`, `mnode::setRoll()`). The **roll function** (`section::rollFunc`, type `funcRoll`) prescribes **roll speed** in degrees per second (or per metre on straight and curved sections), not the roll angle; the angle is the integral. **Roll orientation** (`section::bOrientation`): `QUATERNION` measures roll relative to the track so a flat turn stays unbanked; `EULER` measures it against world up so a turn's yaw contributes banking (the term `glm::dot(vDir, -Y) * fYawFromLast` in `secforced::updateSection()`).
- **Pitch and yaw.** Pitch is the climb angle of `vDir` from the horizontal, yaw its compass heading (`mnode::getPitch()`, `getDirection()`, degrees). **Pitch change** and **yaw change** in degrees per second are what a *geometric section* prescribes (`secgeometric`, functions typed `funcPitch` and `funcYaw` but stored in the `normForce`/`latForce` slots).
- **Normal force, vertical G (y-accel).** The force the seat pushes on the rider, in multiples of g (`mnode::forceNormal`; +1 sitting still on flat track, 0 in free fall, negative is airtime pushing into the restraint). `F_G` = 9.80665 m/s² in `lenassert.h`. The info panel calls it y-Accel.
- **Lateral force, lateral G (x-accel).** The sideways force along `vLat`, in g (`mnode::forceLateral`; for its sign against the standards' axes see `docs/research/r3-force-limits.md`, section 1). The info panel calls it x-Accel.
- **Transition.** One piece of a force, roll, pitch or yaw function: a start value, a change, a duration and a shape (`subfunc`, `core/subfunction.h`). **Transition types** (`enum eDegree`): linear, quadratic, cubic, quartic, quintic, sinusoidal, plateau, tozero and freeform; the quadratic, quartic and quintic ones have a symmetric and an asymmetric flavour chosen with `arg1`. **Time warp**: `centerArg` shifts where the change happens, `tensionArg` sharpens or flattens it (`subfunc::applyCenter()`, `applyTension()`). A **locked** transition stretches to the end of its section. **tozero** is the experimental "roll back to zero" shape that solves for the roll speed that returns the banking to zero by the end.
- **Argument: time or distance.** Whether a forced or geometric section's functions run over seconds or metres (`section::bArgument`, `TIME`/`DISTANCE`; `updateSection()` versus `updateDistanceSection()`).
- **Speed, energy, friction, resistance.** Each node carries `fVel` and `fEnergy` (specific energy, ½v² + g·height). `track::fFriction` is a rolling loss per metre of track, `track::fResistance` an air drag proportional to v³ (the energy lines in each `updateSection()`). A section with `bSpeed` off holds a constant `fVel` (lift hill, launch, brake) instead of coasting.
- **Flexion.** Track curvature: angle change per metre between consecutive nodes (`mnode::fFlexion()`), shown by the Track Flexion visualisation and plot.
- **Smoothing.** A moving-average filter over roll speed between two nodes, kept as `smoothHandler` entries and applied after every recompute once the smoothing dialog was opened or the project loaded (`track::applyRollSmooth()`, `track::bAutoSmooth`). `smoothNormal`/`smoothLateral` on a node are the forces the smoothed track would feel.
- **Track style.** The rail cross-section drawn in the view (`enum trackStyle` in `core/track.h`: generic, generic flat, Vekoma, B&M, triangle, box, small flat, double spine). Display only; it does not change the geometry or the export.
- **POV mode.** Riding the track in the 3D view (`glViewWidget::povMode`, `povPos`); B toggles the NoLimits 1 building border, F fullscreen.
- **Exporter types.** *NoLimits 2* writes a `.nl2elem` text spline (`track::exportNL2Track()`); *Tangent* and *Spline* write a binary NoLimits 1 `.nlelem` made of Bézier segments (`exportTrack4()` and `exportTrack3()`). **Segment length** (metres per exported node) and the **RelRoll threshold** (the angle at which the exporter marks a segment's roll as relative rather than absolute) are the two export knobs.
- **`.fvd` / `.bak`.** The binary project file and the autosave written before every export (`docs/codebase-map.md`, "Saved project file").
- **glm, GLEW, lib3ds, QCustomPlot.** The maths library (vectors, quaternions), the OpenGL extension loader, the `.3ds` writer and the plotting widget. See "Build system and dependencies" in the map.
